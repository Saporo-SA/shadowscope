ShadowScope

Project

ShadowScope was developed by Elbert Santos as a personal project aimed at assisting research on Azure and Microsoft 365, as well as accelerating red teaming and penetration testing efforts. It enables security professionals to identify accessible resources and potential attack vectors based on their current permissions.

Currently, ShadowScope is maintained and further developed by SAPORO as a component of their offensive security toolkit.
